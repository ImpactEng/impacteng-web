Working on now

October 2026

I build, and talk openly about what I find, within what my contracts allow.

  • A blueprint for running Cloud and Platform Engineering as a product. What the function owns, what it costs, and how you know it's working, measured rather than assumed.
  • A shared AI platform for regulated organisations. One platform for every team under APRA rules: where data may go, what to ask first, and what AWS billing can and cannot attribute. Read the write-up.
  • A governed way of working with AI agents, run on my own estate. Across more than 60 repositories, agents now work more hours a week than I do. One set of practices, each rated by how well it is proven, keeps the work consistent, and a checker reports where any repository has drifted. Agents work under their own identity and cannot push, publish or approve. Each piece of data has a level that decides which model provider may see it. A fresh agent re-checks the work before it closes.
  • Building a governed path to production. Agents propose changes, and I approve what ships. How I separated the credentials.
