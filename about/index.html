Story

How I got here

What I do best is turn business strategy into engineering that can be acted on: where the opportunity is, where the risk is, where cost has to come down, and how to get there. And I look at the whole system, not one part. At Australia Post that meant weighing cost against what it did to my people: time to learn what the business would need next, the load of running a large estate of tools, and the balance between central control and team freedom. Paved paths, with enough flexibility that people want to use them.

That work spanned over 7.5 years and four roles, over six of them leading Platform Engineering: a developer platform for 650+ users across three clouds, a team whose internal NPS rose from -61 to 52+, and a $2.3M annual Cloud TCO turned into $7.4M in demonstrable value.

In April 2025 I started ImpactEng. I went deliberately wide first, then narrowed to what holds up in practice: running AI coding privately on AWS in Australia, and designing a shared AI platform for regulated organisations. I build in public and write up what I find.

I write the strategy, the roadmap and the code, and I set up the standards your teams keep after I leave.
