Getting business value from Cloud Engineering

Discover how Cloud Engineering drives measurable business value through automation, cost optimisation, and engineering excellence.

Section 01

Introduction

Cloud Engineering is the driving force behind modern business agility, enabling scalable, secure, and innovative solutions. As a dedicated function, it designs and manages cloud systems on platforms like AWS or Azure. By aligning with business objectives and applying mature practices, businesses can get real value from their Cloud Engineering teams. Here’s how.

Section 02

What is a Cloud Engineering function?

Cloud Engineering is a specialised team that architects, deploys, and optimises cloud-based infrastructure and applications. It utilises tools like Terraform, Kubernetes, and cloud-native services to deliver cost-efficient, resilient systems. From automation to security, the function ensures cloud environments support business success whilst adhering to frameworks like Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF) and Well-Architected Framework.

Section 03

Maximising value from Cloud Engineering

To get the most out of Cloud Engineering, businesses should:

  • Align with Objectives: Link your cloud strategy to outcomes like faster market delivery or cost savings. For instance, a fintech firm can migrate payment systems to AWS, ensuring compliance and scalability.
  • Adopt Best Practices: Use Well-Architected Frameworks and Tools to build secure, efficient architectures and conduct reviews to optimise security, costs and performance.
  • Build Dynamic Teams: Combine ITIL for governance, DevOps for automation, and Agile for flexibility. This synergy accelerates deployments and ensures reliability.
  • Embrace Innovation: Use cloud-native AI and analytics services to create smarter products, such as fraud detection for banking or personalised retail experiences.
  • Optimise Continuously: Utilise auto-scaling, cost management tools, and chaos engineering to ensure resilience and savings.
Section 04

Why it matters

  • Cost Efficiency: Pay-as-you-go and optimisation reduce expenses.
  • Scalability: Handle demand spikes without disruption.
  • Innovation: Data-driven insights fuel growth.
  • Resilience: High availability ensures uptime.
Section 05

Conclusion

Cloud Engineering is a strategic powerhouse for businesses. By aligning with objectives, adopting frameworks, fostering high-performing teams, and embracing cloud-native tools, companies can drive efficiency, innovation, and competitiveness. Invest in your Cloud Engineering function today, it is the key to thriving in a digital world.
