Privacy

What this site collects, who processes it, and how to reach me about it.

Forms

What you enter in a form on this site goes to Formspree, which emails it to me, and I use it only to reply. That is your name and email, what you write, and, for the AI Readiness Assessment, your scores, your organisation if you give it, and whether you ticked the box to hear about your result. Forms also carry the door you chose on the home or services page and any campaign tags from the link you arrived by.

The assessment uses Google reCAPTCHA to filter out automated submissions, which sends information about your browser to Google.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits: the pages you view, how you arrived, including any campaign tags in the link, your approximate location and device type, and which parts of the site you use, such as starting a diagnostic, booking a call, sending a form, or which door or services tab you choose. None of it identifies you, and it never includes your diagnostic answers or anything you type into a form.

The site also uses Microsoft Clarity, which records how pages are used (clicks, scrolling) so I can see where they are confusing. Text you type into forms is masked before it leaves your browser.

To opt out of Google Analytics on every site, use Google's opt-out browser add-on, or a content blocker.

In your browser

Both diagnostics save your progress in your browser so you can pick up where you left off; the AI Fluency Check runs entirely there, and your answers never leave your machine. Your browser also keeps your campaign tags and the door you chose for the session, and remembers whether you turned on the examples on the services page.

Contact

To ask what I hold about you, or to have it deleted, email nitin@impacteng.com.au.
