Privacy

What this site collects, who processes it, and how to reach me about it.

Forms

The contact form and the AI Readiness Assessment send what you enter to Formspree, which emails it to me. For the assessment, that is your email, your organisation if you give it, and your scores. Both forms ask, optionally, how you heard about me.

The assessment uses Google reCAPTCHA to filter out automated submissions. reCAPTCHA sends information about your browser and how you use the page to Google.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits: the pages you view, how you arrived, your approximate location and your device type. It also records when someone starts or finishes a diagnostic, clicks to book a call, or sends a form. It never records your answers or what you type into a form.

The site also uses Microsoft Clarity, which records how pages are used (clicks, scrolling) so I can see where they are confusing. Text you type into forms is masked before it leaves your browser.

To opt out of Google Analytics on every site, use Google's opt-out browser add-on, or a content blocker.

In your browser

Both diagnostics save your progress in your own browser so you can pick up where you left off. The AI Fluency Check runs entirely in your browser: your answers never leave your machine.

Contact

To ask what I hold about you, or to have it deleted, email hi@impacteng.com.au.
