Email Spoofing Check

Free, in your browser

Can someone send email pretending to be you?

Type your business domain. I read its public email records and tell you, in plain words, what they say and what to fix first.

One domain at a time, such as yourbusiness.com.au. You can paste a web address or an email address and I will pick out the domain. I do not take lists. It usually takes under 15 seconds.

How this works

What gets looked up

  • Your browser asks Google Public DNS, and Cloudflare's as a backup, for the public records of the domain you type: mail servers (MX), SPF, DMARC, MTA-STS, TLS-RPT, BIMI, CAA and DNSSEC. Anyone on the internet can read these.
  • SPF is the list of servers allowed to send your email (RFC 7208). DKIM is a signature on outgoing mail (RFC 6376). DMARC is the record that tells mail servers what to do with email faking your address (RFC 7489, which has since been replaced by RFC 9989). The newer standard dropped the pct setting, so receivers differ: some apply it, and others treat the policy as applying to all failing mail. MTA-STS makes senders use an encrypted connection to reach you (RFC 8461).
  • One check makes at most 40 lookups for the main records and for following SPF includes (up to 10 levels deep), plus one lookup for each of 49 common DKIM names: at most 89 lookups in all. If Google does not answer, each is asked again at Cloudflare, so at most 178 requests. Each lookup gets 5 seconds in all, shared between the two. A Cancel button stops it.
  • DKIM names are guesses from a fixed list. If your provider uses a custom name, the key can exist and I will not find it.
  • A subdomain can inherit the DMARC record of its main domain, so a "no DMARC" on a subdomain means checking the main domain too.
  • I do not store the domain you type or the result. The domain goes from your browser to the two DNS services, and nowhere else.

Limits

What this does not do

This reads your public DNS records. It is not a security audit or a penetration test.

It does not log in to anything, send email, or visit your website. It cannot see which services really send email as you, only what your records allow.
