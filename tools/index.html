Privacy

How the tools handle data

The small business tools run in the page and store nothing; the two that read public records send the domain you type, and for the lookalike check close variations of it, to public DNS services, as each page explains. The fluency check scores entirely in the page, and your answers never leave your browser. It saves your progress there so you can pick up later. The readiness assessment asks for a work email to show your results, and your scores and email are sent to me. All the tools are free, with no account.
