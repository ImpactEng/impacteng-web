Payment Change Policy Builder

Free, in your browser

A one-page policy for changes to bank details

Answer a few questions and I build a plain policy you can copy, edit and share with your team.

Invoice scams work by changing the account number: a message that looks like it comes from a supplier asks you to pay somewhere new. A written rule that every change is confirmed by phone gives your staff something to point to.

How a change is confirmed

Payments above this amount to a changed account need a second person. Enter 0 and every change needs one.

How this works

What happens to what you type

  • Your browser fills a fixed template with your answers. No AI writes it, and the wording is the same every time for the same answers.
  • Nothing you type leaves this page, and I do not store it.
  • The policy tells staff to report a scam to ReportCyber (cyber.gov.au/report), the Australian Government's cybercrime reporting service, and to Scamwatch (scamwatch.gov.au). Check both are still the right places before you adopt it.

Limits

What this does not do

This writes a starting template and stores nothing. It is not legal advice, so have the policy read by whoever advises you before you rely on it.

It does not look up your domain, check your email, or send what you type anywhere. Site analytics counts that the tool was used.
