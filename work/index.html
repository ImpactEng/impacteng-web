Case story · Cloud Engineering blueprint

Running a Cloud platform as a product

Situation
A large, publicly listed enterprise in postal, logistics and financial services. I led the team that owned the software delivery platform the rest of the organisation builds on, in regulated environments (PCI and ISM). The gap to close was running it as a product leadership could read.
Move
I ran it as an operating system in six parts: a strategy in business language, two synchronised roadmaps (one for engineers, one for stakeholders), an operating model with a RACI and a skills matrix, self-service patterns each with a shared-responsibility model, monthly reporting to the executive, and a cost and ROI view backed by business cases.
Result
Led the developer platform for 650+ users. Internal NPS for my team from -61 to 52+ over four years, how internal customers saw the team as a whole. Cost and ROI: turned a $2.3M annual Cloud TCO into $7.4M in demonstrable value.

Case story · Shared AI Platform blueprint

One AI platform for every team, under APRA rules

Situation
Regulated Australian organisations want one AI platform every team can use. Under CPS 230, APRA's operational risk standard, a single request, such as an assistant in the contact centre, can make that platform part of a critical operation.
Move
I wrote a reference architecture and a method for standing one up and governing it: the capabilities sorted by what most usage needs on day one and what is expensive to get wrong, questions a team answers before it gets access, data residency set per type of data, and the design tested on AWS.
Result
The path from the first request to the twentieth app, written up in public. Testing found a gap in my own residency policy, closed with one extra deny statement.
